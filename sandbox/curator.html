Sprite Forge — Curator

Build named animations out of the detected sprite frames.

🎯 How to use this tool

  1. Load files — pick a sprite sheet PNG and its analyzer JSON from the pickers below. (Files live at Desktop\sprite_lab\.)
  2. Click "+ New Animation" — the cursor turns into a crosshair and a green banner appears at the top.
  3. Click frames in the order you want them. A number badge pops on each clicked frame showing its position.
  4. Click "Done" in the banner when you've added all the frames you want for that animation.
  5. The animation gets its own card below — rename it, tweak FPS/loop, preview, or rearrange frames with ← → buttons.
  6. Repeat for each animation (punch, walk, idle, whatever).
  7. Save at the bottom → downloads hermes.json to your Downloads folder.

💾 Your work auto-saves to browser storage after every change. You can close the tab and come back — reload the same sheet and it'll offer to restore your animations.
